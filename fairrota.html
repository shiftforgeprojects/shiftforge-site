How it works

Fair by design, not by argument

Three simple ideas do all the work.

1

Shared by effort

Every chore has an effort weight. The app shares them out so no one quietly carries more than their share.

2

Your week, at a glance

A tappable Mon–Sun rota. Tap any day to see what you're on, add a chore to it in one tap, and mark it done. Fixed-day jobs (like bins on Tuesday) sit right where they belong.

3

It evens out

Do more one week and you're owed a break the next. The balance carries forward, so fairness lasts.

Who it's for

Any home that shares a to-do list

If more than one person is responsible for keeping a place running, FairRota keeps it even.

Flatmates & shared houses

End the silent scorekeeping. Everyone can see the balance, so it never comes down to who shouts loudest.

Families & couples

Split the real load of running a home fairly, week after week, without nagging or spreadsheets.

Parents & teens

It isn't only chores. Assign study, practice, or sport to kids with a phone — a fair, visible task board the whole family shares.

Anyone sharing a space

Hostels, small teams, care rotas — wherever effort should be shared, the balance keeps it honest.

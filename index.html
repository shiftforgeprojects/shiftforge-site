Approach

How it is built

Three rules everything here is held to.

Fair by design

The software works for you, not on you — no manipulation, no engagement traps. Fairness is the feature.

Small and sharp

One real problem, solved well, beats a bloated suite that does ten things badly. Light enough to use every day.

Honest about the deal

Plain about what a tool does, what it doesn't, and what it costs. No surprises after you've committed.

Let's talk

Want to try the work, share an everyday problem worth solving, or just say hello? Every message gets read.
